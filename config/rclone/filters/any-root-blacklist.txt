# Applies to every job. Listed first. Unrooted patterns only, so it is safe
# whether the source is a volume root or a subfolder.

# --- Credentials ---
# Repeated at the top of each allowlist in case a job is ever composed
# without this file.
- **/client_secrets.json
- .ssh/**
- **/.credentials.json

# --- Files ---
- *.gguf
- *.safetensors

# --- Folders: any depth ---
- Proxy/**
- $RECYCLE.BIN/**
- Recycle Bin/**

# --- Application caches ---
# Excluded for churn, not size: they cost on every run, not just the first.
- Cache_Data/**
- Code Cache/**
- GPUCache/**
- DiskCache/**
- ShaderCache/**
- shader_cache/**
- Blackmagic Proxy Generator/Support/logs/**

# --- High file-count churn ---
# File quantity is the constraint, not bytes: these cost checker time and HDD
# seeks on F:/G: out of proportion to their size.
- storage/permanent/**
- datareporting/**
- saved-telemetry-pings/**
- Background Tasks Profiles/**
- security_state/**
- shader-cache/**
- crashes/**
- minidumps/**
- ComfyUI/logs/**
- EBWebView/**
- .affinity/Common/*/locks/**

# --- Regenerable editor state ---
# Code/User/*.json and snippets/ are kept; only history and workspace state go.
- Code/User/History/**
- Code/User/workspaceStorage/**

# --- REAPER stock resources ---
# Shipped with REAPER and restored by a reinstall. Config, themes and
# third-party JSFX are untouched.
- REAPER/Data/toolbar_icons/**
- REAPER/Data/track_icons/**
- REAPER/Scripts/Cockos/Default_*_theme_adjuster_images/**

# --- Build caches ---
- .vs/**

# --- Lock files ---
# Held open with no sharing mode, so rclone cannot read them, and one
# unreadable file aborts the entire delete phase of a sync with "not deleting
# files as there were IO errors". A single open browser silently blocks all
# cleanup. Not "*.lock" -- that would swallow Cargo.lock.
- parent.lock
- .parentlock
- LOCK

# --- Editor / OS junk ---
- Thumbs.db
- desktop.ini
- .DS_Store
- *.tmp
- *.temp
